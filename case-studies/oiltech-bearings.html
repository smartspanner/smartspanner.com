In a working manufacturing plant, maintenance is not one type of job. It is the fire extinguisher inspection that must not be missed, the lubrication routine that protects a machine, the specialist called in for a difficult breakdown and the evidence an auditor expects to see.

Relationship disclosure

Oiltech Bearings and Smartspanner are sister companies. Patrick Chester is Managing Director of Oiltech Bearings and Founder of Smartspanner. This story explains how the platform is used in a live manufacturing business and how that day-to-day experience helps inform its development.

The starting point: messages and email, but no maintenance system

Before 2020, Oiltech did not have a dedicated system for maintenance. Conversations happened through WhatsApp and email, leaving useful information spread across message histories and individual inboxes.

That could help with an immediate problem, but it did not provide a dependable view of what was due, what had already happened or which records belonged to each machine. For recurring compliance and maintenance work, the business needed a process that would keep operating without relying on somebody remembering the next date.

The practical requirement

Give the team one current view of every scheduled requirement, asset history and supporting record—on the plant floor as well as at a desk.

One system for three layers of manufacturing maintenance

Oiltech implemented Smartspanner across its single Newcastle manufacturing site. The system now brings together compliance activity, preventative routines and the reactive work needed to keep production equipment available.

Compliance

  • Fire extinguisher inspections
  • Risk assessments
  • Emergency-stop audits
  • Gas equipment and space heaters
  • Electrical inspections and PAT testing
  • Lifting-equipment examinations under LOLER

Preventative maintenance

  • Lubrication routines
  • Filter changes
  • Blasting-equipment maintenance
  • EDM wire-cut machine servicing
  • Compressor servicing

Reactive & specialist work

  • Production-equipment breakdowns
  • Work with local service companies
  • OEM support for demanding repairs
  • Warranty records for new machinery
  • Warranty records for used machinery

Each activity has a place in the same asset history. The team can see the next planned task, refer to earlier work and retain photos, service information and evidence without reconstructing the story from old conversations.

Supporting ISO 9001 with evidence of control

Smartspanner plays an important part in Oiltech's ISO 9001 quality management system. It provides a structured way to demonstrate that maintenance and compliance requirements have been identified, scheduled and completed, with an activity history and supporting records available for review.

For external auditors, that makes evidence easier to present. For the people running the plant, the more immediate benefit is confidence that important work remains visible and is less likely to be missed.

Following an asset through its complete working life

The asset record is useful from the moment equipment arrives until the day it leaves service. When Oiltech replaced a grit blaster, the new machine was registered from the plant floor, photographs were added and the outgoing machine was deactivated. The live asset list changed with the physical plant rather than being corrected later from memory.

That same history becomes valuable when a machine fails. Warranty information can be checked, earlier service activity reviewed and the right local specialist or OEM brought into the job. New and second-hand machinery can both be managed with the context needed to make a sensible repair decision.

A manufacturing plant that informs the platform

Patrick's roles in both businesses create a direct feedback loop between software development and day-to-day manufacturing. Smartspanner is used against real deadlines, real equipment and real audit requirements at Oiltech—not only demonstration data.

Experience on the plant floor helps expose practical questions: how quickly an asset can be registered on a phone, whether the right information is available during a breakdown, how evidence is retrieved for an auditor and where routine work can be made harder to overlook. Those lessons help inform the continuing development of the Smartspanner platform.

The result: less dependence on memory, more control

Nothing important disappears into messages

Scheduled work and asset records remain visible in the business instead of being scattered across WhatsApp and email histories.

Audit evidence is easier to present

Plans, completed activity and supporting records help Oiltech demonstrate maintenance control within its ISO 9001 system.

Information reaches the plant floor

The mobile apps give the team access to current asset and maintenance information while they are with the equipment.

Machinery retains its history

Servicing, breakdown activity, photographs and warranties stay connected to the relevant asset throughout its working life.

Built around the realities of manufacturing

At Oiltech, Smartspanner connects routine compliance with the practical work of maintaining a manufacturing plant. It provides the day-to-day control the team needs internally and the structured evidence the business needs when its systems are examined externally.
