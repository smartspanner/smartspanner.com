Running a restaurant seven days a week means maintenance and compliance never stop. Across three locations, Nadon Thai has recurring checks to arrange, equipment to service, certificates to retain and inspection findings to resolve.

The challenge: maintenance should not depend on one person's memory

Before Smartspanner, that workload was managed through a combination of people, paperwork and individual knowledge. As responsibilities changed, it became harder to know that every requirement was being handled consistently across all three restaurants.

The risk was not simply that a task might be missed. When a fire or health inspection took place, the business also needed to show that the right work had been completed and provide the supporting record without searching across paper files, emails and separate reminders.

The underlying problem

The business could not rely on one person knowing what needed to happen, when it was due and where the evidence was stored.

Bringing recurring work into one system

Nadon Thai implemented Smartspanner as the central system for planned maintenance, general repairs and recurring compliance activity across its restaurants.

Fire & safety

  • Fire alarm servicing and testing
  • Fire extinguisher inspections
  • Emergency lighting tests
  • Fire risk assessments
  • Six-monthly extraction cleaning

Gas & electrical

  • Annual CP42 commercial catering gas safety inspection and certification
  • Five-year electrical testing
  • PAT testing

Equipment & premises

  • Boiler servicing
  • Fridge and freezer maintenance
  • Appliance maintenance
  • General building maintenance

Instead of managing each requirement separately, the team can schedule it, assign responsibility, retain the completion record and see what comes next in the same place.

Compliance evidence when it matters

A central record changes what happens when an inspector asks for evidence. Rather than reconstructing the history from paperwork or individual inboxes, Nadon Thai can bring up the relevant maintenance and compliance information from Smartspanner.

The record shows what was scheduled and completed, with supporting information attached to the activity. That gives the team a clearer way to respond during inspections and a reliable history to refer back to afterwards.

Turning inspection findings into action

An inspection record is only useful if the issues it identifies are followed through. Nadon Thai uses Smartspanner to turn findings into visible, assigned maintenance actions that can be tracked through to completion.

The result: greater control across all three restaurants

Smartspanner gives Nadon Thai a consistent maintenance and compliance process across the group. Recurring work is visible, supporting records are easier to retrieve and inspection findings have a clear route to completion.

Evidence is ready

Records and supporting information can be accessed when fire, health or other inspections take place.

Knowledge stays with the business

Schedules and records no longer depend on one person remembering every requirement.

Actions stay visible

Issues raised during inspections become trackable work instead of disconnected notes.

Every location follows one process

Planned work, general maintenance and compliance activity are managed consistently across the group.

Maintenance that does not depend on memory

For Nadon Thai, Smartspanner is more than a place to record maintenance. It holds the recurring requirements of the business, shows what needs attention and preserves the evidence of what has already been done.
