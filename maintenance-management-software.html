What is Maintenance Management Software?

Maintenance management software helps organisations plan, track, and control maintenance activities across assets, equipment, and facilities.

It provides a central platform to:

  • Manage maintenance tasks and workflows
  • Schedule preventive maintenance
  • Track assets and equipment
  • Monitor maintenance performance
  • Improve team coordination

Modern systems like Smartspanner combine all of these capabilities into one easy-to-use solution.

The Problem with Traditional Maintenance Management

Many organisations still rely on disconnected tools and manual processes.

This often results in:

  • Reactive maintenance instead of planned maintenance
  • Poor visibility into maintenance activities
  • Inefficient use of resources
  • Increased equipment downtime
  • Inconsistent record-keeping

Without a structured system, maintenance becomes difficult to manage and optimise.

Maintenance Management Software vs CMMS

The terms are often used interchangeably, but there is a subtle difference:

Maintenance Management Software CMMS
Broader term for maintenance systems Specific type of maintenance software
Covers all maintenance activities Focuses on computerised management
May include multiple tools All-in-one platform
