What is a CMMS?

A CMMS (Computerised Maintenance Management System) is software designed to help organisations manage maintenance operations, assets, and work orders in one central platform.

It enables maintenance teams to:

  • Schedule and track work orders
  • Manage preventive maintenance plans
  • Monitor asset performance
  • Control spare parts inventory
  • Improve compliance and reporting

Modern CMMS platforms, like Smartspanner, are cloud-based and mobile-friendly — allowing teams to manage maintenance from anywhere.

CMMS vs EAM — What's the Difference?

CMMS (Computerised Maintenance Management Systems) focus on maintenance operations — work orders, preventive maintenance, and asset tracking.

EAM (Enterprise Asset Management) systems are broader and often more complex, covering full asset lifecycle management across large enterprises.

For many organisations, a modern CMMS provides:

  • Faster implementation
  • Lower cost
  • Better usability
  • More focused functionality
