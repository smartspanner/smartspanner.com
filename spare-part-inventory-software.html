Inventory Management Questions

What is intelligent perpetual inventory counting?

It is an ongoing cycle-counting programme that prioritises stock using time since the last count, category priority, inventory value and the maximum permitted interval between checks. It distributes verification across normal working days instead of relying on one disruptive annual stocktake.

How is the daily inventory-check requirement calculated?

Smartspanner calculates the workload from the number of eligible stock records, their required counting intervals and the site's configured working week. There is no daily target for an administrator to maintain manually.

How does Smartspanner calculate Inventory Accuracy?

Inventory Accuracy compares expected quantities with physically counted quantities from the last 90 days. It is quantity-based, so a larger discrepancy affects the result more than a small one.

What affects Inventory Health?

The score considers overdue counts, negative stock, parts without a category, items below their minimum stock level and missing BIN locations when BIN compliance has been enabled.

Can inventory be compared across countries and currencies?

Yes. A site can show its inventory value in its configured currency, while organisation-wide Inventory Performance converts site values into the Company Profile base currency for consistent comparison.
