What Is Preventive Maintenance Software?

Preventive maintenance software schedules inspection, servicing and replacement work using a planned interval or trigger. When the next activity is due, the plan creates a work order that can be assigned, completed and recorded against the asset.

The schedule is only the beginning. A connected preventive maintenance workflow should:

  • Define the asset, task, frequency, procedure and responsible person
  • Create the next job from a date, meter reading or condition trigger
  • Give the technician the information needed at the equipment
  • Record notes, photographs, parts, time and findings on completion
  • Update asset history and make the next due work visible

Keep the record without slowing down the work.

Planned Work Competes with Today's Breakdown

Teams often know which routine work matters, but the plan is spread across spreadsheets, calendars, service labels and individual memory. Urgent demand then pushes important work out of view.

The operational risk is not only a missed date. It is also:

  • No clear owner for the next activity
  • Frequencies that are not reviewed as equipment use changes
  • Technicians searching for the latest procedure or earlier findings
  • Completion evidence stored away from the asset history
  • No reliable basis for deciding whether the plan is effective

Types of Preventive Maintenance Schedule

Meter-Based Maintenance

Condition-Based Maintenance

Event and Seasonal Plans

Preventive and Reactive Maintenance Both Have a Place

Preventive maintenance is planned before a failure; reactive maintenance responds after an issue occurs. Neither approach should be applied universally.

Consider preventive maintenance whenRun-to-failure may be rational when
Failure affects safety, compliance, production or qualityFailure has low consequence and does not create another hazard
A known task can manage a credible failure modeThe item is inexpensive, accessible and quick to replace
Manufacturer, warranty or operational requirements specify servicingPlanned intervention would cost more than the consequence it manages

Frequently Asked Questions

What is preventive maintenance?

Preventive maintenance is planned work carried out at an intended interval or trigger to manage a failure mode, meet a servicing requirement or keep an asset in its required condition. It can include inspections, lubrication, cleaning, adjustment and planned replacement.

Should every asset have preventive maintenance?

No. The strategy should reflect asset criticality, failure consequence and whether a planned task can manage the relevant failure mode. Run-to-failure can be reasonable for low-consequence items that are inexpensive and quick to replace.

How often should preventive maintenance be scheduled?

Frequency depends on the asset, failure mode, usage, operating environment, manufacturer information, legal duties and maintenance history. Smartspanner supports time, meter and condition-based triggers so plans can reflect the chosen strategy.

Can preventive maintenance work orders be created automatically?

Yes. A Smartspanner maintenance plan can create the next work order when its time, meter or condition trigger is reached. The work order carries the asset, task and assignment information into the completion workflow.
