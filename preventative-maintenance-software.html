What is Preventive Maintenance Software?

Preventive maintenance software helps organisations plan and schedule maintenance activities before equipment fails.

Instead of reacting to breakdowns, teams can:

  • Schedule recurring maintenance tasks
  • Track asset condition and service history
  • Automate work orders
  • Ensure maintenance is completed on time

By moving from reactive to proactive maintenance, businesses can reduce downtime, improve reliability, and extend asset lifespan.

The Problem with Reactive Maintenance

Relying on reactive maintenance creates serious operational risks:

  • Unexpected equipment failures
  • Increased downtime and lost production
  • Higher repair costs
  • Shortened asset lifespan
  • Disrupted schedules and workflows

Without a structured preventive maintenance system, teams are constantly firefighting issues instead of preventing them.

Types of Preventive Maintenance

Preventive maintenance can be applied in several ways.

Time-Based Maintenance

Scheduled at regular intervals — daily, weekly, or monthly.

Usage-Based Maintenance

Triggered based on equipment usage — hours, cycles, or output.

Condition-Based Maintenance

Triggered when asset condition changes — using IoT or monitoring data.

Smartspanner supports all of these approaches, allowing you to build the right strategy for your operation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is preventive maintenance?

Preventive maintenance is maintenance performed regularly to reduce the likelihood of equipment failure. It includes scheduled inspections, servicing, and part replacements carried out before problems occur.

Is preventive maintenance worth it?

Yes — preventive maintenance reduces downtime, lowers long-term repair costs, and improves asset reliability. Organisations that move from reactive to preventive maintenance typically see significant improvements in operational efficiency.

How often should maintenance be scheduled?

This depends on the asset, its usage patterns, and manufacturer recommendations. Smartspanner supports time-based, usage-based, and condition-based scheduling so you can tailor the frequency to each asset.

Can preventive maintenance be automated?

Yes — Smartspanner automates scheduling and work order generation. Once you set up a recurring maintenance plan, the system automatically creates and assigns work orders when they are due.
