Clear Sales and Support Expectations

The app interface is multilingual. Sales, onboarding, and customer support are currently provided in English from our UK team.

Language Interface, Not Local Office Coverage

Smartspanner is a UK-based CMMS provider. We do not currently operate local sales offices in Germany, France, Spain, Portugal, or Brazil, and we do not position these language options as local market branches.

The multilingual capability is designed to help existing and new customers support maintenance users who prefer a German, French, Spanish, or Portuguese app interface.
