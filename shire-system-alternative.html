Where ShireSystem may be the better choice

We don't believe every company needs the same CMMS.

ShireSystem offers extensive maintenance and facilities management capabilities and can also support on-premise deployment.

Its dedicated resource planning and document controls may matter if you need detailed workload planning, document version histories and expiry controls. Its Expert offering also supports more extensive configuration.

If these capabilities are central to your requirements, ShireSystem may be the better fit. Check the relevant edition and deployment options with Eleco.

Smartspanner is designed for organisations that want strong maintenance management functionality without turning their CMMS into another system that needs managing. If your team needs everyday maintenance control and fewer administrative steps, include it in your shortlist.

ShireSystem alternative FAQs

Is Smartspanner an alternative to ShireSystem?

Yes. Both are CMMS platforms covering assets, work orders, planned maintenance and other maintenance processes. Their approach, feature depth and pricing structure differ. Smartspanner is worth considering if you want a simpler approach with published per-user pricing; compare your required workflows before deciding.

Can I move from ShireSystem to Smartspanner?

Existing maintenance data can be prepared for migration where suitable exports are available. Contact Smartspanner so we can assess the asset register, available files and history. Not every field or historical record will necessarily map automatically; agree the migration scope first.

How much does Smartspanner cost?

Smartspanner starts at {{ entry_plan.currency }}{{ entry_plan.price }} per user/month, excluding applicable taxes. See current plans and included features.

Does Smartspanner support multiple sites?

Yes. Multi-site deployment is included in all plans. Manage assets and maintenance across locations in one system. See multi-site maintenance capabilities.

Does Smartspanner have a mobile app?

Yes. Native iOS and Android apps give technicians access to work orders, assets, QR codes, photos and maintenance updates. The apps require an internet connection and do not work offline. Initial signup and setup take place in a web browser. Read about mobile access.

Does Smartspanner have an API?

Yes. API access is included on Advanced. Use it to connect maintenance data to reporting and other business systems, including solutions built with Power BI or Excel. Explore API and integration information.

Is Smartspanner suitable for manufacturing?

Yes. It is used by manufacturing and engineering organisations for preventive maintenance, breakdowns, asset history and inspection records. Read the AMES Goldsmith UK and Oiltech Bearings stories to see examples. Oiltech Bearings and Smartspanner are sister companies.
