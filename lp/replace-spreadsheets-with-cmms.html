The hidden cost of spreadsheet-based maintenance

01

Missed Preventive Maintenance

Without automated scheduling, planned maintenance slips through the cracks — leading to reactive firefighting and equipment failure.

02

Poor Job Tracking

No clear visibility into who's doing what, when it was completed, or what's still outstanding across your team.

03

Increased Downtime

Reactive workflows and missed maintenance directly increase unplanned downtime and emergency repair costs.

04

Inefficient Labour Usage

Without proper scheduling, technicians waste time on low-priority tasks while critical work goes unassigned.

05

No Scalability

Spreadsheets that work for 10 assets break down at 100. Growth demands a system that scales with you.

06

No Data-Driven Decisions

Without centralised reporting, you're making decisions based on gut feel instead of real maintenance data.
