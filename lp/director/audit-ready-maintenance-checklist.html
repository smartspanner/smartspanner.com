What's Inside the Checklist

A practical, no-fluff walkthrough of exactly what is commonly requested across:

PUWER

Machine guarding and work equipment checks.

LOLER

Lifting equipment records, intervals, and evidence.

PSSR

Pressure systems checks and written scheme evidence.

COSHH

Hazardous substances, LEV checks, and inspection records.

Electrical Safety

EICR, fixed wire testing, and supporting maintenance evidence.

Fire Risk

Fire risk assessments, actions, renewals, and proof of follow-up.
