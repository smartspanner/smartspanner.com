Keep Maintenance Work Moving

Work orders are the operational record of maintenance: what needs doing, who owns it, what asset it relates to, which parts were used, and when the work was completed.

Smartspanner replaces scattered requests, emails, paper notes, and spreadsheets with a single work order workflow that technicians and managers can update from desktop or mobile.

How Smartspanner Work Orders Work

Use work orders for reactive jobs, planned maintenance, inspection follow-up, safety actions, and improvement work.

Create the Job

Raise a work order with the asset, site, Critical, High, Medium or Low priority, description, instructions, and files the technician needs.

Assign Responsibility

Allocate work to individuals or teams so ownership is visible from request to completion.

Track Progress

Follow job status, updates, parts used, labour, and completion evidence in real time.

Build Service History

Every completed work order becomes part of the asset and maintenance record.

Give Every Work Order a Clear Priority

Set every job as Critical, High, Medium or Low so technicians and managers can distinguish immediate risks from routine work without relying on notes, spreadsheets or local conventions.

Critical

Immediate safety, compliance or operational risk requiring urgent attention.

High

Urgent maintenance work that should receive prompt attention.

Medium

Important work managed through the team's normal planning process.

Low

Routine, minor or improvement work that can be planned accordingly.
