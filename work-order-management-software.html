What is Work Order Management Software?

Work order management software is used to create, manage, and track maintenance tasks from start to completion.

It allows maintenance teams to:

  • Create and assign work orders
  • Track job progress in real time
  • Record labour, parts, and time
  • Maintain a complete service history
  • Improve communication across teams

A modern work order system replaces manual processes like spreadsheets, paper forms, and emails with a centralised, efficient platform.

The Problem with Manual Work Order Systems

Many organisations still rely on outdated methods to manage maintenance tasks.

This leads to:

  • Lost or delayed work orders
  • Poor communication between teams
  • Lack of visibility into job status
  • Incomplete maintenance records
  • Inefficient use of technician time

Without a proper system, maintenance becomes reactive, disorganised, and difficult to scale.
