        How this assessment works

        We look at maintenance complexity, observed control gaps, business impact and implementation readiness. Headcount, spreadsheets or a large asset register alone do not establish a reason to buy. Unknown or conflicting answers lead to a request for more evidence.

        The approach is informed by the US Department of Energy’s CMMS needs guidance and HSE guidance on inspection records. Neither source endorses this tool or sets its decision rules. The categories are practical judgements, not scientifically validated scores. Software does not establish compliance.

        Smartspanner fit uses its documented features and access requirements, reviewed September 2026. Specialist integrations, IoT, validation and governance requirements need detailed review.
