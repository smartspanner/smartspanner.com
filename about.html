Est 2013

We built the maintenance system we needed ourselves

Smartspanner began with a practical manufacturing problem. Patrick Chester was responsible for operations across sites in three countries, where preventive maintenance was too easily displaced by breakdowns and changing priorities. The available systems did not fit the way a smaller operational team actually worked.

The first version was built for that environment: cloud-based, accessible wherever the work was happening and focused on keeping maintenance visible without adding unnecessary administration. It gave the existing process a more reliable home instead of asking the team to redesign its operation around the software.

Smartspanner was founded in 2013 to make that approach available to other businesses. The first external customer began using the platform in early 2015, and customer feedback has shaped its development ever since.

Today, Smartspanner supports businesses ranging from small maintenance teams to multi-site organisations. The capability has grown, but the product discipline remains the same: keep the common workflow short and put the advanced capability within reach.
