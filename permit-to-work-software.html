Control High-Risk Work

A permit-to-work system is used to authorise and control higher-risk jobs before work starts. In UK maintenance environments, permits are commonly used for hot work, confined spaces, electrical isolation, work at height, breaking containment, excavation, and other jobs where hazards, precautions, isolation, competence, and handover need clear control.

Smartspanner's permit to work module is built directly into the CMMS, so permits do not sit in a separate spreadsheet, PDF folder, or paper binder. They can be linked to maintenance work, assigned to one or more Smartspanner users, signed on mobile or tablet, exported as a PDF for wet signatures, emailed to stakeholders, and stored with the rest of your maintenance evidence.

For the wider audit-ready compliance story across permits, inspections, fire risk assessments, work orders, and reports, see maintenance compliance software.

The workflow is aligned with UK permit-to-work good practice, including the principles described in HSE HSG250, while leaving your competent person, risk assessment, site rules, and approval process under your organisation's control.

Common Permit to Work Use Cases

Use Smartspanner permits to work for the controlled tasks that need explicit authorisation before maintenance starts.

Hot Work

Welding, cutting, grinding, brazing, and other spark or heat-producing work where fire precautions and post-work checks matter.

Confined Space Entry

Controlled entry where atmosphere testing, rescue arrangements, isolations, and authorised personnel must be clearly recorded.

Electrical and Mechanical Isolation

Support isolation controls alongside Smartspanner's lockout tagout software and asset records.

Work at Height

Document planned controls, competent people, equipment checks, exclusion zones, and permit acceptance before work begins.

Breaking Containment

Control work on pipework, tanks, vessels, pressure systems, and process equipment where stored energy or substances may be present.

Excavation and Site Works

Record authorisation, location, services checks, and site precautions for controlled work around buildings, yards, and plant areas.

From Permit Request to Closed Record

Smartspanner helps teams move permit-to-work control out of paper packs and into a repeatable CMMS workflow.

  1. Create the permit with job details, site, asset, work order, users, controls, and supporting files.
  2. Review and authorise the permit before work starts, using your site's approval rules and competent people.
  3. Capture signatures on mobile or tablet, or export a PDF when a printed wet signature is required.
  4. Share the record by email with the people who need visibility, including contractors and managers.
  5. Close and archive the permit with the linked maintenance record so evidence is retained for audit.
