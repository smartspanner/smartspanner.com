The maintenance team knew what needed to be done, but planning it meant working across several Excel spreadsheets. Assigning jobs took time, progress was difficult to follow and producing engineering reports meant manually bringing information together again.

The starting point: several spreadsheets and a manual planning process

Before Smartspanner, different elements of the maintenance record lived in different Excel workbooks. Planning and assigning jobs required significant administration, while due dates, expected durations and work in progress were difficult to see as one workload.

The same fragmentation affected reporting. Collating engineering and KPI information was slow, making it harder to identify problem areas and decide where the plant needed more maintenance attention.

The underlying problem

The team had maintenance data, but it was spread across separate files and had to be manually assembled before it could guide the next decision.

Giving every job an owner, duration and due date

Smartspanner replaced the separate planning records with one current view of reactive work, preventive maintenance, contractor activity and statutory inspections.

Jobs can now be assigned with an expected duration, priority and due date. The team can see outstanding work immediately, while the person planning maintenance can follow work in progress without updating another spreadsheet.

Log work Set priority and duration Assign Complete and update Review workload and KPIs

Because updates are visible across the plant in real time, the maintenance team can concentrate on the highest-priority work first. Managers can access the same current record rather than searching across workbooks or waiting for a separate update.

More than 1,100 jobs in the first 18 months

Since completing the rollout approximately 18 months ago, the business has logged more than 1,100 maintenance jobs in Smartspanner. Many are reactive tasks, reflecting the day-to-day reality of maintaining an operating manufacturing plant.

Alongside that work, the team has established more than 20 time-based preventive maintenance tasks that recur automatically. New activities are being added each week as the business develops a more comprehensive preventive maintenance schedule.

Reactive work

  • Plant issues logged as they arise
  • Priority visible to the team
  • Work in progress tracked centrally

Preventive maintenance

  • More than 20 recurring tasks
  • Time-based work created automatically
  • Schedule expanding each week

Contractor & statutory work

  • Contractor activity in the same workload
  • Statutory inspection due dates
  • Advance reminders for preparation

An estimated 8–10 hours of administration saved each week

The customer estimates that Smartspanner saves approximately 8–10 hours each week across scheduling work, managing the team's workload, preparing engineering reports and collating KPI data.

The time saving is one part of the result. The shared workload also makes priorities clearer and provides faster access to the information used to identify recurring plant problems.

Turning KPI data into a view of plant priorities

With completed work held in one system, KPI information no longer has to be rebuilt from several spreadsheets. The customer can collate engineering reports more quickly and use the resulting data to see which areas of the plant require more focus.

Technicians also have a live view of outstanding work and its priority. This connects the management report to the jobs the team sees, rather than maintaining separate operational and reporting records.

Preparing for pressure vessel examinations in good time

The move from Excel has also changed how the business manages statutory inspections. In the past, pressure vessel examination dates were recorded in a spreadsheet. The process depended on somebody opening the correct file and noticing that an examination was approaching.

When a date was missed, the insurance company's reminder could leave the team scrambling to prepare the equipment at short notice.

Those examination due dates are now held in Smartspanner. The customer chooses how far in advance the reminder should be issued, giving the team time to prepare the equipment before the inspection is due.

Record examination date Choose reminder period Receive advance reminder Prepare equipment Complete examination

Smartspanner supports the customer's inspection-planning process by keeping the due date and reminder visible. Responsibility for defining and meeting the applicable statutory requirements remains with the customer and its competent advisers.
