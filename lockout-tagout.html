Lockout and Tagout Register in Smartspanner

The lockout feature in Smartspanner allows advanced plan users to track asset lockout/tagout. With a custom list of locks and reasons for lockout, you can track active lockouts on the dashboard of both mobile and web apps.

Ensure safety compliance and prevent accidental equipment startup during maintenance with comprehensive LOTO tracking and documentation.

For higher-risk jobs that need formal authorisation before work starts, Smartspanner also includes Permit to Work software with mobile signature capture, PDFs for wet signatures, email sharing, and linked CMMS users.
