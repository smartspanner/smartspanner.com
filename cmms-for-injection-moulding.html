The Challenges in Injection Moulding Maintenance

Injection moulding operations face unique maintenance challenges:

  • Mould wear and cavity damage
  • High cycle counts requiring frequent servicing
  • Unplanned machine downtime
  • Cooling system and hydraulic failures
  • Quality issues caused by poor maintenance
  • Difficulty tracking mould and machine history

Without a structured system, these challenges lead to increased scrap, downtime, and cost.
