Last updated: 27 October 2026

Your choices

Optional analytics, session replay and marketing tools stay off until you consent to their category. “Reject optional” keeps all three off. “Choose settings” lets you select categories individually. Necessary storage continues to support the website and services you request.

Your choice is valid for 180 days on this browser. After that, we ask again. You can change it earlier using Cookie settings in the footer. Withdrawing consent stops optional tracking, clears accessible optional cookies and reloads the page. Browser controls can also remove or block storage.

Necessary storage

  • smartspanner_cookie_consent: first-party local storage recording your choices, the notice version and when you chose them. We validate the choice for 180 days; local storage itself remains until removed or replaced.
  • smartspanner-maintenance-assessment: session storage used to save your assessment in the current tab. It contains your answers and recommendations and is not sent to our servers. It ends when the tab session ends or you restart the assessment.
  • Security and delivery providers may use necessary cookies to protect requested services, such as a form submission, from abuse.

Optional analytics

With analytics consent, Google Analytics 4 measures website use and confirmed form actions. Its _ga and _ga_* cookies distinguish browsers and maintain session information. Google's default duration is up to two years; browser limits and configured settings can shorten this.

Our first-party session storage may record a recent customer-story origin and a successful form submission so the correct conversion is counted once on its matching thank-you page. These records have a 30-minute validity window. They contain event and page identifiers, not form answers or contact details.

Optional session replay

With separate replay consent, Contentsquare helps us understand browsing interactions. Cookies may include _cs_id (up to 13 months), _cs_s (30 minutes) and _cs_c (up to 13 months). The tool can reproduce page interactions, so form controls are masked and replay is disabled on enquiry, resource-download and assessment pages.

Optional marketing

With marketing consent, HubSpot can associate website activity with a browser and, after identification, an enquiry. Its cookies may include hubspotutk and __hstc (up to six months), __hssc (30 minutes) and __hssrc (session).

When configured, Google Ads, LinkedIn and Meta tags also require marketing consent. Advertising cookies can include Google's _gcl_* (typically 90 days), Meta's _fbp/_fbc and LinkedIn identifiers. Exact cookies and lifetimes depend on the enabled integration, your choices and the provider's settings.

With marketing consent, smartspanner_campaign session storage can retain campaign parameters and advertising click IDs for up to 30 minutes, to attach to a submitted enquiry where the HubSpot form supports those fields. Rejecting marketing leaves those hidden fields empty.

Forms, videos and the assessment

Optional-cookie consent is not required to submit an enquiry or request a resource. The information you submit is sent to HubSpot to provide that service. HubSpot's behavioural tracking script requires marketing consent.

Videos are loaded when you choose to play them. Their providers receive the technical information needed to deliver the video. The assessment page does not load analytics, marketing tracking or session replay; its answers stay in your tab.
