Who we are

Smartspanner Ltd is registered in England and Wales, company number 08592005. Our registered office is 6 Queens Court North, Third Avenue, Team Valley Trading Estate, Gateshead, Tyne and Wear, England, NE11 0BU. Contact support@smartspanner.com about privacy.

This notice describes information handled through our website and service. Smartspanner is the controller of website enquiries and business contact information. When a customer uploads maintenance information for its organisation, that organisation controls the information and we process it to provide the service. A Data Processing Addendum is available on request.

Information you provide

When you request a demo, contact us or download a resource, we receive the fields you submit, such as your name, business email, telephone number, requirements and message. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in free-text fields.

Customer accounts contain profile and business details and information entered into the service, such as assets, maintenance jobs, procedures and records of completed work. We use this information to operate the service, provide support and administer the customer relationship.

Website visits and optional tools

Necessary storage supports features you request and remembers your cookie choices. Optional analytics, replay and marketing scripts stay off until you consent to the relevant category. You can reject optional tools, choose individual categories or change your choice through . See our Cookie Policy for storage names and durations.

  • Google Analytics 4: with analytics consent, measures page visits, device and browser information, approximate location and interactions such as successful form submissions. We use it to understand website use and improve the enquiry journey. Cookie identifiers can distinguish browsers; this information is not described as anonymous. We do not send visitors’ names, email addresses, telephone numbers or assessment answers as analytics event parameters.
  • Contentsquare: with session-replay consent, collects browsing interactions, page views, device information and session identifiers to help us identify usability problems. Session replay can reproduce page interactions. We mask form controls and disable replay on enquiry, download and assessment pages. We do not use the replay tool to collect completed form answers.
  • HubSpot: receives details you deliberately submit in our forms so we can respond to your enquiry, provide your requested resource and manage the business relationship. Form submission works without optional-cookie consent. With marketing consent, the HubSpot tracking script also records website activity and browser identifiers, which can be linked to a contact record. Campaign parameters and advertising click identifiers may be attached to enquiries when marketing consent and the relevant form configuration are in place.
  • Advertising integrations: Google Ads, LinkedIn and Meta tags, when configured, require marketing consent. They can use page activity, advertising identifiers and selected conversion events to measure campaigns and support personalised advertising. Only confirmed demo and trial conversions are eligible for our advertising conversion workflow.

Embedded product videos load when you choose to play them. The video provider receives the network and device information needed to deliver the video. A link to an external site is governed by that site's privacy information.

The maintenance assessment

Assessment answers and recommendations stay in the browser tab's session. They are not sent to Smartspanner or HubSpot. The assessment does not load analytics or session recording. Requesting the full report sends your first name, email and report-request identifier to HubSpot; the report opens in your browser.

How and why we use information

We process personal information under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018. Our purposes and lawful bases include:

  • Responding to an enquiry and taking steps requested before a contract: pre-contract steps, or our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries as appropriate.
  • Providing and administering a subscription: performance of the contract and, where applicable, our legitimate interests in business administration and security.
  • Maintaining accounting records and responding to lawful requests: legal obligations.
  • Optional website measurement, replay and advertising: consent. You can withdraw this at any time through Cookie settings.

Submitting an enquiry does not itself subscribe you to a marketing newsletter. We use contact details to provide the requested response or resource and manage related business enquiries. Any separate marketing permission can be withdrawn.

Providers and international processing

We use service providers to operate the website, supply the service and handle enquiries, including Google, Contentsquare and HubSpot as described above. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect the security of the service.

Providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where required, applicable transfer safeguards must be in place, such as adequacy arrangements or approved contractual safeguards. Contact us for information about processors and transfers relevant to your use of the service.

Provider information: Google privacy, Contentsquare privacy and HubSpot privacy.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry and customer information for as long as needed to respond, provide the service, manage the business relationship and meet legal requirements. The retention period depends on the record and its purpose. Contact us to ask about a particular record.

The Terms of Service explain account termination and the 30-day export window. Customer data may be deleted after that period unless further retention is required. We use access controls and security measures to protect information; you should also protect your account credentials.

Your rights and choices

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent. Contact support@smartspanner.com. If your information is controlled by a customer organisation, we may direct your request to that organisation.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. We update this notice when our processing changes and will provide further notice where required.
