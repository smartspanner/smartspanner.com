What is Asset Management Software?

Asset management software helps organisations track and manage physical assets throughout their lifecycle.

This includes:

  • Equipment and machinery
  • Facilities and infrastructure
  • Tools and mobile assets

With a centralised system, teams can:

  • Monitor asset performance
  • Track maintenance history
  • Schedule servicing
  • Manage asset data and documentation

Modern asset management software integrates with maintenance workflows — giving complete visibility across operations.

The Problem with Poor Asset Management

Without a structured system, asset management becomes fragmented and inefficient.

Common challenges include:

  • Lack of visibility into asset condition
  • Missing or incomplete maintenance records
  • Unplanned equipment failures
  • Difficulty tracking asset history
  • Inefficient use of maintenance resources

This often leads to increased downtime, higher costs, and reduced asset lifespan.
