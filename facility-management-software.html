What is Facility Management Software?

Facility management software helps organisations manage buildings, infrastructure, and maintenance operations efficiently.

It provides a central platform to:

  • Manage maintenance tasks and work orders
  • Schedule preventive maintenance
  • Track assets and equipment
  • Monitor facility performance
  • Ensure compliance and safety

Modern facility management systems bring all operations together — improving visibility, coordination, and control.

The Challenges of Facility Management

Managing facilities involves coordinating people, assets, and maintenance activities across multiple locations.

Common challenges include:

  • Reactive maintenance and unexpected failures
  • Lack of visibility across sites
  • Poor communication between teams
  • Manual processes and paperwork
  • Difficulty tracking compliance and inspections

Without the right system, facility management becomes inefficient and difficult to scale.
